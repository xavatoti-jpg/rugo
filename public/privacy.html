Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

What we collect

When you create an account with Google or Facebook, Rugo receives your email address, name and profile picture. We also store the username you choose. We never receive your password.

Messages you post in chat rooms are public to everyone in that room. They are stored for 7 days and then deleted automatically.

How we use it

Only to let you sign in, show your username in chats, and keep the community safe (for example, blocking spam and abuse). We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Services we use

Supabase (accounts and sign-in), Render (website hosting), and Google or Facebook (sign-in only).

How to delete your data

You can ask us to delete your account and all your data at any time. Email contacto@rugo.chat with the subject "Delete my Rugo account" from the email address you signed up with. We will delete it within 30 days.

If you used Facebook, you can also remove Rugo's access in Facebook → Settings → Apps and Websites.
